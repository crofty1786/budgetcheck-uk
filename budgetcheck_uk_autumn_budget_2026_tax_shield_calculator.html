What Actually Changed in the Autumn Budget 2026?

A clear, non-partisan summary of the four biggest measures affecting UK households in 2026/27 and April 2027.

From 6 April 2027

Savings Interest Tax +4%

Interest earned outside an ISA above your Personal Savings Allowance (£1,000 basic / £500 higher / £0 additional) rises from 20%/40%/45% to 24%, 44%, and 49%.

Impact: Hits savers with >£22k at 4.5% AER
From 6 April 2027

Rental Property Tax +4%

Individual landlords in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland move to dedicated property income tax rates of 24% (basic), 44% (higher), and 49% (additional).

Impact: +£400 tax per £10,000 net rent
Retained in Full

£20,000 Cash ISA Limit Kept

Proposals to cut the Cash ISA cap to £12,000 for under-65s were scrapped. All UK adults retain the full £20,000 annual tax-free ISA allowance.

Opportunity: Shield £20k every 6 April
Frozen Until 2031

Thresholds & Fiscal Drag

The £12,570 Personal Allowance and £50,270 Higher Rate threshold remain frozen, pulling more workers into 40% Income Tax and halving their savings allowance.

Relief: Standing charge reforms & energy bill support

Three Legal Ways to Beat the 2027/28 Tax Squeeze

How UK savers and higher-rate taxpayers use existing HMRC allowances before the 5 April tax year deadline.

1

Bed & ISA Before 5 April

With savings tax hitting 24% to 49% in April 2027, holding cash in a standard taxable bank account becomes far less efficient. Moving up to £20,000 into a flexible Cash ISA locks in tax-free compounding permanently.

2

Reclaim Your £1,000 Savings Allowance

Earning just £1 over £50,270 cuts your Personal Savings Allowance from £1,000 to £500 and hikes your savings tax rate from 24% to 44%. Making a gross pension contribution brings your Adjusted Net Income back under £50,270.

3

Escape the 60% £100k–£125,140 Taper

For every £2 earned over £100,000, you lose £1 of your £12,570 Personal Allowance—creating an effective 60% Income Tax rate (plus 2% NI). Pension contributions restore your Personal Allowance pound-for-pound.
